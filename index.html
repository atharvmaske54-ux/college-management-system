COURSE OUTCOME 3 DEADLOCK MONITOR

Hall-Resource Deadlock Detection

Simulates Resource Allocation Graphs (RAG) and circular wait dependencies between halls, proctors, and student batches.

Operating System Coffman Conditions for Deadlock

THEORY & VIVA
1. Mutual Exclusion: An examination hall or invigilator cannot be shared simultaneously by two independent batches.
2. Hold and Wait: Batch B1 holds Hall H-01 while waiting for Invigilator I-02 to arrive.
3. No Preemption: Resources (hall key, paper vault token) cannot be forcibly confiscated until the batch concludes.
4. Circular Wait: H-01 waits for I-02 ➔ I-02 waits for H-02 ➔ H-02 waits for I-01 ➔ I-01 waits for H-01.
